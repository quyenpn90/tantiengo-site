TanTien Go

The driver app for TanTien Corp and partner transport companies. Everything a driver needs — orders, vehicle balance, and shift handover — in one place.

📦 Delivery Orders

Receive and track delivery orders by date, with route, customer, and cargo details.

💰 Vehicle Balance

Track the outstanding balance of your assigned vehicle in real time.

🔄 Shift Change

Hand over your vehicle to another driver with admin-approved shift-change requests.

💸 Money Transfers

Create money transfer requests between vehicles, pending admin approval.

🔔 Instant Notifications

Get notified the moment a new order is assigned to you.

🚚 For Fleets

Partner carriers can contact us to onboard their fleet and drivers.

Note: TanTien Go is intended for drivers with an account issued by their transport company. Please contact your fleet manager for login credentials.
